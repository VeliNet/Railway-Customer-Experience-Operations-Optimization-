# Railway-Customer-Experience-Operations-Optimization-
"Improving Railway Efficiency, Revenue, and Customer Satisfaction Using Data” 
A national railway corporation is trying to improve operational efficiency, reduce  delays, and increase revenue. 

🚆** Railway Performance Analysis**

**📌 Problem Statement**

Railway systems face challenges with delays, cancellations, revenue fluctuations, and changing customer behaviour.
**
The goal of this project is to:**

Understand what drives revenue
Identify causes of delays and inefficiencies
Analyse customer behaviour and booking patterns
Reduce refund rates
Improve overall service reliability and customer satisfaction
**
📊 Dataset Description**

The dataset contains railway journey records, including:

Transaction ID
Departure Time & Date
Departure & Arrival Stations
Journey Status (On-Time, Delayed, Cancelled)
Delay Reasons (Weather, Technical Issues, etc.)
Ticket Price (Revenue)
Purchase Type (Online / Offline)
Payment Method (Card, Cash, etc.)
Refund Request (Yes / No)

This dataset helps analyse operations, revenue, and customer trends.

**🧹 Data Cleaning Process**

The following steps were taken to prepare the data:

Removed missing and duplicate records
Standardised text values (e.g., “Delayed”, “Cancelled”)
Created new calculated columns:
Route (Departure → Arrival)
Departure Hour (extracted from time)
Time Category (Peak vs Off-Peak)
Ensured correct data types (date, time, numeric)
Verified consistency in refund and journey status fields

**📈 Analysis**

The analysis focused on three key areas:

1. Revenue Analysis
Total revenue generated from ticket sales
Revenue trends across peak and off-peak periods
Average revenue per journey

3. Operational Performance
Delay rate and cancellation rate
On-time performance
Identification of major delay causes
High-risk routes with frequent disruptions

5. Customer Behaviour
Online vs offline purchase trends
Preferred payment methods
Relationship between delays and refund requests

**🔍 Key Insights**

💰 Revenue
Revenue is highest during peak travel periods
Ticket sales directly drive overall revenue

⚙️ Operations
Most delays are caused by:
Weather conditions
Technical issues
Certain routes experience higher disruption rates

👥 Customer Behaviour
Customers strongly prefer digital payments (cards)
Online booking is increasing and improves convenience

💸 Refunds
Refunds are mainly linked to:
Delayed journeys
Cancelled journeys

**🚀 Recommendations**

🚆 Reduce Delays
Improve maintenance to reduce technical issues
Monitor high-risk routes closely
Use weather forecasting for better planning

💰 Optimize Pricing
Apply dynamic pricing during peak hours
Offer discounts during low-demand periods
Encourage early bookings

📱 Increase Online Purchases
Promote online booking with incentives
Improve user experience for faster transactions
Support more digital payment options

💸 Reduce Refund Rates
Minimise delays and cancellations
Offer rescheduling options instead of refunds
Improve communication during disruptions

😊 Improve Customer Satisfaction
Provide real-time journey updates
Ensure reliable and timely service
Simplify booking and payment processes

📊 Business Impact
Increased revenue through better pricing and peak optimisation
Improved operational efficiency by reducing delays
Higher customer satisfaction through better service and digital experience
Reduced financial losses from fewer refunds


✅ Final Note

This project demonstrates how data can be used to:
Improve decision-making
Enhance customer experience
Optimize operations in the railway industry
