# Railway-Customer-Experience-Operations-Optimization-
**Improving Railway Efficiency, Revenue, and Customer Satisfaction Using Data**
A national railway corporation is trying to improve operational efficiency, reduce  delays, and increase revenue

**PROBLEM STATEMENT**
Railway systems face delays, cancellations, changing customer behaviour, and unstable revenue. The goal is to understand revenue drivers, reduce delays, and improve customer experience.

**ANALYSIS**
Revenue is higher during peak travel periods. Most delays are caused by weather and technical issues, and some routes are more affected than others. Customers prefer online booking and digital payments. Refunds mostly happen when trains are delayed or cancelled.

**KEY INSIGHTS**
Peak periods generate the most revenue. Delays are mainly due to weather and technical faults. Online booking and card payments are now the most used options. Delays and cancellations are the main reasons for refunds.

**RECOMMENDATIONS**
Improve maintenance to reduce technical issues. Monitor high-risk routes and plan better using weather forecasts. Use dynamic pricing by increasing prices during peak periods and offering discounts during low demand. Promote and improve online booking. Offer rescheduling instead of refunds and provide real-time updates to customers.

**TOOLS AND TECHNIQUES USED**
Power BI and Excel were used for analysis and dashboards. Key techniques include data cleaning, trend analysis, comparison of customer behaviour, and identifying links between delays and refunds.

**BUSINESS IMPACT**
Better pricing will increase revenue. Fewer delays will improve efficiency. More online bookings will speed up transactions. Reduced refunds will prevent revenue loss. Improved service will increase customer satisfaction and loyalty.
